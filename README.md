## What is Qalbanion?

**Qalbanion** (*from Qalb, the Arabic word for heart*) is a private, personal journal designed as a calm, lifelong companion for your heart and mind.

Most modern note-taking apps and journals have turned into productivity dashboards—pressuring you with daily streak counters, reminder notifications, to-do lists, and complex folder hierarchies. 

Qalbanion was created as the antidote to that pressure. It is designed to feel like opening a beautiful, cloth-bound notebook. You write when clarity is needed, whether that is every day, once a week, or once a month. **No streaks. No targets. No guilt.**

## Key Highlights

### ✍️ Effortless, Distraction-Free Writing
Open Qalbanion and start writing immediately. You do not need to name a title, pick a category, or assign tags before putting pen to paper. A thought can be two sentences or two thousand words.

### 💾 Keystroke-Level Autosave (Works 100% Offline)
Every keystroke saves instantly to your computer's local memory. If your laptop battery dies, your browser closes, or you lose Wi-Fi on an airplane, **you will never lose a single word**. When you reconnect to the internet, your thoughts sync quietly and securely to your private cloud.

### 🌿 "Quiet Editorial" Design
Inspired by classic print literature and personal memoirs:
- **Warm Ivory & Cream Palette**: Soft, calming paper tones that are gentle on the eyes.
- **Deep Charcoal Typography**: High-contrast, elegant serif headlines paired with clean, readable prose.
- **True Dark Mode**: Designed for late-night journaling before sleep.

### 🧭 Gentle Reflections & "On This Day"
- **"On This Day" Moments**: Surfacing past thoughts written on the same calendar date in previous seasons to help you notice how much you have grown.
- **Guided Reflection Templates**: Thoughtful, optional prompts for daily reviews, weekly retrospectives, and tough decisions you want to think through calmly.

### 🗂️ Thematic Collections & Tags
Group your thoughts into natural streams of inquiry without rigid folders:
- *Personal Growth*
- *Ideas & Creative Sparks*
- *Questions I Haven't Answered Yet*
- *Things I Want to Revisit*

### 📊 Thought Evolution & Unresolved Questions
- **Thought Evolution**: Place two entries written months apart side-by-side to observe how your opinions, priorities, or concerns have changed over time.
- **Unresolved Questions**: A dedicated space to keep track of open paradoxes and life questions you are pondering, without turning them into a stressful to-do list.

### 🔒 100% Private, Safe & Truly Yours
- **No Ads, No Tracking**: Your thoughts belong exclusively to you.
- **Complete Data Ownership**: Export your entire journal anytime with a single click to **Markdown (.md)**, **Plain Text (.txt)**, or **JSON**. You will never be locked into our app.
- **Optional AI Assistant**: If you ever want AI help discovering themes in your writing, it requires your explicit, entry-by-entry permission. Qalbanion will never automatically read or train on your journal.

## For Those Who Want to Run It on Their Own Computer

If you want to run your own local copy of Qalbanion on your machine:

1. **Download the code**:
   ```bash
   git clone https://github.com/justcooking/Qalbanion.git
   cd Qalbanion
   ```

2. **Install the dependencies**:
   ```bash
   npm install
   ```

3. **Start the journal**:
   ```bash
   npm run dev
   ```
   Open your browser and visit `http://localhost:5173`. That's it!
